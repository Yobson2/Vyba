# 📱 Nightlife Discovery & Booking App – Description

This mobile application aims to enhance the nightlife experience in local communities by connecting users with nearby entertainment venues such as bars and “maquis.”

The platform is built around two main types of users: **clients** and **venue owners**. Venue owners (bar or maquis managers) can create and manage their establishment profiles, publish promotions, and share events that are displayed in a dynamic news feed accessible to users. This enables them to increase their visibility and attract more customers in real time.

On the user side, clients can easily discover nearby venues based on their location, explore current offers, and access essential information such as opening status and seating availability. The application also allows users to reserve places in advance, providing a smoother, more organized, and reliable experience when going out.

By centralizing venue discovery, promotion, and booking into a single platform, this application simplifies how people find and enjoy local nightlife while offering venue owners effective tools to grow their business.
